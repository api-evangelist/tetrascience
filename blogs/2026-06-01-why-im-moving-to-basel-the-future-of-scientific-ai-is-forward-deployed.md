TetraScience Co-founder and Field CTO Siping "Spin" Wang is relocating to Basel to launch the company's European headquarters, arguing that the future of Scientific AI requires being forward-deployed in life sciences superclusters to bridge cultural gaps and accelerate discovery. Wang introduces the concept of "Sciborgs" — a new class of hybrid operators who combine scientific domain expertise with AI capabilities. The move reflects TetraScience's broader strategy to embed deeply within global pharmaceutical and biotech ecosystems.
